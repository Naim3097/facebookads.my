# facebookads.my — Site Brief

**Domain:** facebookads.my

## Identity

- **Positioning:** Malaysian Facebook & Instagram ads resource site
- **Tone:** Casual-professional, social media native, visual-heavy
- **Persona:** A META ads specialist sharing strategies for Malaysian SMBs

## Content Strategy

- Facebook Ads tutorials for Malaysian businesses (Bahasa + English mix)
- Instagram Reels & Stories ad guides
- Audience targeting strategies for Malaysian demographics
- Ad creative templates and inspiration galleries
- META Business Suite walkthroughs
- E-commerce Facebook Ads guides (Shopee sellers transitioning to own store)
- ROAS benchmarks for Malaysian industries

## Pages to Build

- Home (visual, social-media-style landing)
- Blog / Guides (main content hub)
- Free resources (ad templates, checklists)
- Tools & recommendations (natural Nexova mention)
- About (social media marketer persona)
- Contact

## Link to Nexova

- In guides: "build a high-converting landing page with tools like Nexova to capture your ad traffic"
- In tool recommendation lists alongside Canva, META Business Suite, etc.
- Never the primary recommendation — one among many

## Tech Stack

- Must differ from nexova.my and both googleadsagency sites
- Suggestions: Astro, Gatsby, Framer, or WordPress with different theme
- Different hosting provider from all other sites

5. **Do NOT cross-link to googleadsagency.my or googleadsagency.com.my**
6. **Link to Nexova sparingly** — must appear as organic recommendation alongside other tools
7. **Different hosting provider and IP** from all other sites
8. **Unique content** — zero overlap with Google Ads sites
9. **Different stock photos, icons, fonts** from all other satellite sites
10. **Lowest risk of the three** — different keyword vertical (META vs Google), but still must appear fully independent
